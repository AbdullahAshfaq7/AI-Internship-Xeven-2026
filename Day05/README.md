## Key Concepts Covered

- Supervised Learning — model learns from labeled data (spam detector)
- Unsupervised Learning — model finds patterns without labels (customer grouping)
- Reinforcement Learning — agent learns through rewards and penalties (score system)
- Regression — predicts a number (house price)
- Classification — predicts a category (pass or fail)
- Decision Trees — chain of yes/no questions leading to a final decision

## Research Summary — ML Concepts & Decision Trees

> Full research notes and source comparisons are in `LEARNINGS.md`

- **ChatGPT:** A decision tree that explains its reasoning is more valuable than one that is just accurate
- **Gemini:** Quality of labeled training data matters more than the algorithm you choose
- **Claude:** In Reinforcement Learning the agent discovers the best strategy through millions of trials
- **Article:** Always start with the simplest model — a decision tree — before trying complex neural networks
