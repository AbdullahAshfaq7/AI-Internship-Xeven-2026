### What is Artificial Intelligence?

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is when computers are programmed to think and
learn like humans. Instead of following fixed rules, AI systems learn from
data and improve over time.

#### History:

1950s — Alan Turing asked "Can machines think?" and created the Turing Test
1980s–90s — AI winters (hype died, funding dried up)
2012 — Deep learning exploded, computers started beating humans at image recognition
2022–now — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — AI is everywhere

#### Real World Examples:

- **Netflix** recommends shows based on what you watched
- **Gmail** automatically filters spam from your inbox
- **Doctors** use AI to detect diseases from X-rays faster
- **Banks** use AI to detect fraud before you even notice

Think of AI as the big umbrella. Under it:

│       ├── NLP — understands and generates text (ChatGPT)

ML — you show it 1000 cat photos, it learns what a cat looks like
Deep Learning — a more powerful version of ML using layers of neurons
NLP — the field YOUR internship focuses on (LangChain, RAG, chatbots)
Computer Vision — how phones unlock with your face


4. ### Role of an AI Engineer in 2026

An **AI Engineer** is the person who builds and maintains these intelligent
systems. Their job includes:

- **Collecting data** — gathering information for the AI to learn from
- **Training models** — teaching the AI using that data
- **Testing** — making sure the AI makes correct decisions
- **Deploying** — making the AI available in real apps and products
- **Maintaining** — monitoring and improving the AI over time

In simple terms, if AI is the brain, the AI Engineer is the one who
builds, trains, and takes care of that brain.

+You're not training models from scratch — you're building products on top of existing AI.

5. ### Training vs Inference

**Training:** 

- Teching The Model
- Companies like OpenAI Do It Once.
- Cost: Millions of Dollar
- You dont do it.

**Inference**

- Using The Model
- Done Every Time You Call API
- Cost: Cents Per Call
- You do it and Every API is Called Inference.

2. ## Gemini
**Key Points:** AI is a computer that helps humans do things smarter and faster
**Examples:** Google Search, detecting fraud in bank, translating languages
**Clarity:** 4/5
**Best Insight:** AI is a helper tool, not a replacement for humans

3. ## Claude
**Key Points:** AI learns from tons of data and finds patterns to make predictions
**Examples:** Detecting cancer in X-rays, blocking spam, face unlock on phone
**Clarity:** 5/5
**Best Insight:** Bad data = bad AI. Data quality matters most
